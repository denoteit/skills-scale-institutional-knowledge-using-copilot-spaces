# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Executive Sponsors

### Role Summary
Executive Sponsors provide organizational sponsorship and ensure projects remain aligned with strategic objectives. They make or escalate decisions that exceed the project team's authority without taking over day-to-day delivery coordination.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm strategic goals, desired outcomes, and measures of success
- Secure organizational support and help resolve resource or policy barriers
- Make or escalate decisions that are outside the Project Manager's authority
- Review major risks, progress, and decisions requiring leadership attention

### How They Work with Other Roles
- Align with Product Managers on business outcomes and strategic priorities
- Partner with Project Managers on escalations, major risks, and delivery status
- Provide direction and remove organizational barriers so Developers and the wider team can deliver

---

## Business Analysts

### Role Summary
Business Analysts clarify business needs and translate them into actionable, traceable requirements and acceptance criteria.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit and document business needs, workflows, constraints, and requirements
- Trace requirements to intended outcomes and identify gaps or ambiguity
- Surface requirement changes, assumptions, and dependencies
- Support clear acceptance criteria and shared understanding of requested work

### How They Work with Other Roles
- Work with Product Managers to refine requirements and inform backlog prioritization
- Coordinate with Project Managers on requirement dependencies, decisions, and changes that affect plans
- Answer Developers' questions and clarify how requirements apply to implementation

---

## UX / User Researchers

### Role Summary
UX / User Researchers investigate user needs and validate proposed experiences so product decisions reflect evidence about users.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and conduct appropriate user research and usability evaluation
- Identify user needs, barriers, and feedback relevant to proposed workflows
- Share findings and recommendations with the team
- Consider accessibility and usability as part of experience validation

### How They Work with Other Roles
- Work with Product Managers to connect user evidence to product priorities and success measures
- Collaborate with Developers to assess feasibility and accessibility of proposed experiences
- Coordinate research timing and design handoffs with Project Managers

---

## Technical Leads / Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads / Architects guide technical design and integration to support reliable, maintainable delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Guide technical approaches, architecture, and engineering quality standards
- Identify cross-component dependencies, constraints, and technical risks
- Support design reviews and resolve technical questions across the team
- Advise on technical trade-offs and implementation sequencing

### How They Work with Other Roles
- Collaborate with Developers on design, implementation, and technical reviews
- Advise Product Managers on feasibility and technical trade-offs that affect product choices
- Share estimates, dependencies, and technical risks with Project Managers for planning and tracking

---

## QA / Test Leads

### Role Summary
QA / Test Leads coordinate quality planning and provide evidence that work has been validated against its expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Define a fit-for-purpose test strategy and identify quality risks
- Coordinate validation and report test results, defects, and readiness evidence
- Work with the team to make requirements and changes testable
- Track quality issues through resolution or explicit risk acceptance

### How They Work with Other Roles
- Partner with Developers on testability, validation, and defect resolution
- Align with Product Managers on acceptance expectations and product quality
- Coordinate with Project Managers on quality gates, delivery status, and release risks

---

## Release / Operations Owners

### Role Summary
Release / Operations Owners coordinate deployment and operational readiness so changes can be launched, monitored, and supported safely.

### Responsibilities
- Plan deployment and operational readiness, including monitoring and rollback
- Coordinate post-release support and communicate operational constraints
- Confirm release plans and readiness evidence are available to the team
- Surface deployment risks, incidents, and support needs

### How They Work with Other Roles
- Coordinate with Developers and Technical Leads on release changes and operational requirements
- Work with QA / Test Leads on verification and release readiness evidence
- Align with Product Managers on user-facing impact and rollout expectations
- Keep Project Managers informed about timing, dependencies, readiness, and escalations

---

## Stakeholders / Customer Representatives

### Role Summary
Stakeholders / Customer Representatives provide domain knowledge and represent the needs of affected users or business functions.

### Responsibilities
- Share relevant business context, user needs, constraints, and feedback
- Participate in discovery and reviews when their input is needed
- Validate whether proposed outcomes address the needs they represent
- Raise changing needs or concerns through agreed project channels

### How They Work with Other Roles
- Provide user and business input to Product Managers for discovery and prioritization
- Participate in reviews and provide decisions or feedback requested by the team
- Receive project status, decisions, and updates through the Project Manager's communication plan
- Work with Business Analysts to clarify domain needs and requirements where appropriate

---

## Role Assignment and Accountability
- A person may hold more than one role, especially on a small team; the roles describe accountabilities, not required job titles or separate staffing positions.
- Make decision ownership, handoffs, and escalation paths explicit for each project so responsibilities do not fall between roles.
